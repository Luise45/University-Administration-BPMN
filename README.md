
# University Semester Management Process (BPMN)

![BPMN](https://img.shields.io/badge/BPMN-2.0-blue)
![Camunda](https://img.shields.io/badge/Camunda-Modeler-orange)

## Project Overview

This BPMN 2.0 model was created to represent the administrative processes involved in managing university teaching operations throughout an academic semester.

The model focuses on how teaching requirements are planned, staffing needs are identified, schedules are created, and personnel are compensated after the semester concludes.

---

##  Main BPMN Process

The main process consists of three participating pools:

* **University Management**
* **Teaching Staff**
* **Module Coordinators**

<img width="3430" height="1480" alt="Main BPMN Process" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5bd7a0e6-cee6-43c6-a498-2db65a37bee5" />

University Management acts as the central coordinator of the process. Their responsibilities include:

* Determining teaching requirements for the upcoming semester
* Identifying available teaching staff
* Recruiting new personnel when staffing shortages occur
* Coordinating schedules and contractual agreements

---

##  Personnel Planning & Recruitment

One of the core subprocesses focuses on evaluating staffing requirements and initiating recruitment when necessary. If existing personnel cannot cover all required courses, new teaching staff are identified and hired.

##  Timetable Creation & Contract Management

After the initial timetable draft and recruitment activities have been completed, the final schedules are prepared. Contracts are then generated and distributed to the assigned teaching personnel.

At the end of the semester, the process concludes with the calculation and issuance of personnel payments.

---

##  Key Subprocesses

The model is structured around four major subprocesses:

### 1. Initial Timetable Draft
Creation of the first semester schedule based on module requirements and available teaching staff.

### 2. Personnel Recruitment
Identification and hiring of additional personnel when staffing shortages are detected.

### 3. Final Timetable Preparation
Adjustment and finalization of teaching schedules after staffing decisions have been completed.
### 4. Personnel Payment
Processing and issuing payments for teaching staff following the completion of the semester.
